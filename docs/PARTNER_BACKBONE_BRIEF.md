# Partner Intelligence Backbone — Brief

A concise read for a partner architect arriving at the public demo for the first time. Aim: under two minutes to decide whether the cloud-agnostic mapping framework on `/v2/partner-backbone` is a useful candidate map for your engagement.

## In one sentence

A cloud-agnostic architecture layer that maps urban evidence, geospatial intelligence, AI explanation, governance, and buyer readiness onto partner-ready deployment pathways.

In Gulf-institutional Arabic: *طبقة معمارية محايدة سحابيًا تربط الأدلة الحضرية، الذكاء الجغرافي، الشرح الذكي، الحوكمة، وجاهزية المشترين بمسارات شراكة قابلة للتنفيذ.*

## What the surface shows

Four tabs. Each is a different reading of the same advisory architecture map.

### A. Intelligence Workflow (A → I)

A nine-step trace of how an evidence pack becomes a reviewer-facing readiness signal:

| # | Step | One-line description |
|---|---|---|
| A | Evidence Intake | Reference urban-evidence packs registered for review. |
| B | Classification | Tokens organised into source families and intent categories. |
| C | Transform | Projected onto the analytical surface; no persistent DB, no live ETL. |
| D | Buyer Intelligence | Composed into buyer-side framings (municipality, investor, insurance, telecom, cloud, advisory). |
| E | Satellite / Earth Evidence | Public-basemap spatial context; zone-level posture, never individuals. |
| F | Mathematical Core | Bounded readiness bands; weights frozen until governance review. |
| G | AI Explanation | Reviewer-facing rationale tied back to source tokens. |
| H | Decision Surface | Bands + evidence + explanation in one institutional view. |
| I | Governance | Digest-verified audit trail; municipal review remains the sole producer of consequential conclusions. |

Each step renders a description, an input/output pair, and a governance caveat where one is constitutional.

## What this is NOT

* Not an active partnership claim with any cloud, telecom, advisory, or municipal vendor.
* Not a production deployment claim. The demo runs on Vercel for visual review only.
* Not a yield model or investment recommendation. The platform never issues a guarantee.
* Not an automated decisioning system. Municipal review is the sole producer of consequential conclusions.
* Not a substitute for the partner's own due diligence. The capability map is an advisory architecture mapping; the partner brings the operational rigour.

## How to read this with your team

1. Open `/v2/partner-backbone` in a browser.
2. Switch between EN and AR via the language toggle. Confirm both languages render naturally (no broken bidi, no machine-translation artefacts).
3. Walk the four tabs. Identify which generic capability categories your firm can supply or audit.
4. Read the constitutional boundary line at the top and bottom of the page. If your engagement preserves the framing of "candidate mapping only · no active integration," there is a starting point for a conversation. If your engagement requires a claim that violates the boundary, the public demo is not the right channel.
5. Use [`GOVERNANCE_BOUNDARY.md`](GOVERNANCE_BOUNDARY.md) and [`PUBLIC_DEMO_DISCLAIMER.md`](../PUBLIC_DEMO_DISCLAIMER.md) for the full constitutional read.
